How to order

  1. Choose your size. Check the size guide. Our tees are cut oversized on purpose.
  2. Place your order. Send a DM to @emk_system on Instagram or email emksystem01@gmail.com with your size and quantity.
  3. Confirm your order. We confirm availability, pricing and shipping details with you before you pay.
  4. Payment. Payment instructions are sent privately once your order is confirmed.
  5. Shipping. Once payment is received and verified, your order is processed and shipped, and you get tracking information.

We only take orders through @emk_system and emksystem01@gmail.com. Never send payment to anyone else, even if they use our name or photos.

Shipping

  • Processing time. Orders are processed once payment has been received and confirmed.
  • Shipping cost. Shipping is paid by the customer. We tell you the cost before you pay.
  • Tracking. We share tracking information once your order has shipped.

Returns and exchanges

Every EMK piece is designed with intention. Our tees have an intentionally oversized fit, so please check the size guide before you order.

All sales are final. We do not offer refunds.

Size exchanges

  • Exchanges are accepted within 14 days of delivery, subject to size availability.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition, with all tags attached.
  • The customer pays all shipping costs, including shipping the item back to us and shipping the replacement.
  • We do not provide prepaid return labels or free exchange shipping.

To start an exchange, DM @emk_system or email us with your name, order details and the size you want.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, or you receive the wrong item, contact us at emksystem01@gmail.com or on Instagram. These cases are handled separately from the all sales final policy and in line with applicable consumer protection laws.

Privacy

This website. There are no accounts, forms, cookies, ads or analytics. The Peace Protocol keeps your access ID, the words you unlock, the size you pick and your sound setting in your own browser's storage, on your device only. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

When you order. Orders happen on Instagram or by email, so those services' own privacy policies apply to your messages. We use the details you send us, such as your name, shipping address and contact information, only to confirm, ship and support your order. We do not sell your information.
